Privacy Policy

Doc To Pdf (Android app, package com.preet.doctopdf)
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what the Doc To Pdf app does with your files and what information is collected when you use it. In short: your documents and photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded by us. The app is free and shows ads, and it uses Google services for ads, usage statistics and crash reports.

1. What the app does

Doc To Pdf lets you choose documents, images or PDF pages, scan documents with the camera, crop and clean them, edit text, merge pages, and save the result as a PDF. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

2. Your files and photos

  • You pick files and photos with the Android system picker, so the app only gets the items you choose.
  • Conversion, cropping, filters, merging and text recognition (OCR) all run on your device. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit with a model that is bundled inside the app, so your photos and the text found in them are not sent anywhere.
  • Finished PDFs are saved on your device (for example in your Downloads folder or the place you choose). We do not receive a copy.
  • A small list of your recent documents and your app settings are kept only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only when you tap the option to take a photo or scan a page. The photo stays on your device.
  • Internet and network state: used to show ads, load ad consent forms, and send crash reports and usage statistics.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google’s advertising and analytics services (see section 4).
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): used to save the PDF into your Downloads folder. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

4. Information collected by third-party services

The app includes the following services. They collect information automatically, in line with their own policies.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) shows banner, interstitial and app-open ads. According to Google, it collects and shares your IP address (which can be used to estimate a general location), interactions with ads and the app, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.
  • Google User Messaging Platform shows a consent form where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) and records your choice.
  • Firebase Analytics collects an app-instance ID and basic usage events, such as screens viewed and sessions, to help us understand how the app is used.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and basic device information (such as device model and Android version) so we can fix crashes.

Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information for advertising and Firebase privacy information.

We do not sell your files or your personal information, and we do not read the contents of your documents or photos.

5. Your choices

  • Ad consent: where a consent form is shown, you can accept or decline personalised ads.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • Your files: you can delete any saved PDF from your device at any time, and from the app’s document list.
  • Uninstall: removing the app deletes everything it stores on your device.

6. Children

Doc To Pdf is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app’s features or the services it uses change, we will update this page and the date at the top. Please check it from time to time.
